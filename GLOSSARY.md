# Walk-in Record Brief

This glossary names the clinical information used to prepare a doctor-facing brief from records a patient brings to a visit.

## Language

**Prior record**:
A prescription, report, discharge summary, or other health document created before the current consultation and brought or supplied by the patient.

**Record brief**:
A concise, visit-focused account of prior records, arranged by date and linked to the material from which its facts came.
_Avoid_: Clinical note, diagnosis

**Source document**:
The patient-supplied paper page or digital file from which a brief fact originates.

**Needs review**:
A status for information that cannot be read or resolved confidently and requires a person to check the source document.

**Doctor-reviewed finding**:
A source-linked finding the current doctor has checked and accepted for possible use in the clinical note. Review of a patient statement does not independently establish that the statement is true.
_Avoid_: Doctor-confirmed fact

**Patient-reported medicine use**:
The patient's answer about whether a named medicine is being taken at the current visit: taking, stopped, or unsure. A past prescription does not establish this status.

**Partial record brief**:
A record brief shown while some supplied pages are still processing or some paper has not yet been captured. Its visible findings are limited to ready source documents.

**Quarantined page**:
A captured page withheld from the patient's record brief because its patient identity does not match the visit being reviewed.

**Draft note selection**:
A finding deliberately chosen by the doctor for the current consultation's unsigned note. A new or changed finding does not enter the draft automatically.

**Review trail**:
A dated history of patient-reported corrections and doctor review decisions retained for later interpretation.

**Clinical note**:
The doctor's record of the current consultation, including assessment and management.
_Avoid_: Record brief

**Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)**:
India's national framework for identifying people and exchanging health records among participating systems with consent.
_Avoid_: A single national EHR

**ABHA number**:
A national health identifier used to link a person's records across participating ABDM systems with the person's consent. The identifier itself is not a collection of all prior records.
_Avoid_: ABHA record

**eHealth Kerala UHID**:
The patient identifier issued by Kerala's eHealth system for care records and services at participating government hospitals.
_Avoid_: ABHA number

**Health Information Provider (HIP)**:
An ABDM participant that creates or holds health information and can share it digitally through the network.

**Health Information User (HIU)**:
An ABDM participant that requests access to health information with the patient's informed consent.
