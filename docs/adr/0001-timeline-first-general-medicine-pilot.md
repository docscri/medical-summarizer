# Timeline first for the General Medicine pilot

The doctors consulted preferred Prototype 2's chronology for reviewing prior records, so the General Medicine pilot opens on the timeline. An earlier research recommendation favored a brief-first opening; the doctors' reported preference supersedes it for this pilot, while the brief and split-screen variants remain available for comparison. The timeline decision does not establish measured time savings or error rates, which the clinic pilot must evaluate before a production layout is fixed.
