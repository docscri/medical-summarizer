# Kerala EHR and ABDM integration research

Research checked: 12 October 2026. Scope: the proposed General Medicine walk-in record brief for a potential private clinic in Kerala. This is an integration research note, not a claim that the clinic or its patients already use any of these systems.

## Names and boundaries

| Term | Verified meaning for this project |
| --- | --- |
| eHealth Kerala UHID | Kerala eHealth issues a Unique Health Identity (UHID) for its state system. Its public portal describes longitudinal records and appointments at **eHealth-enabled government hospitals**. The Kerala Health Department also describes access to prescriptions and lab results through that portal. These statements do not establish that a private clinic can query the state system. | ABHA | The Ayushman Bharat Health Account identifier is part of the national Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM). It identifies a person and helps link records across participating systems with informed consent. **The ABHA number is not a repository containing all of a patient's records.** ABDM describes a federated system in which records remain with record holders and are exchanged with consent. [eHealth Kerala ABDM page](https://www.ehealth.kerala.gov.in/abdm), [NHA privacy and security explanation](https://abdm.gov.in/static/media/Session%206%20-%20Digital%20health%20-%20Privacy%20%26%20Security.083a5b3e73ba533e321b.pdf) |
| ABHA app / PHR | A personal health record app can help a person discover, link, view, and consent to sharing available records. The official app privacy policy says records may be fetched for display with consent and cached or downloaded on the phone. This is different from assuming every historical record is available through an ABHA lookup. [eHealth Kerala ABDM page](https://www.ehealth.kerala.gov.in/abdm), [NHA app privacy policy](https://abdm.gov.in/static/media/New_Privacy_Policy.3833de7c114b64627a9d.pdf) |
| HIP / HIU | A Health Information Provider creates or holds shareable records; a Health Information User requests access to another holder's records with informed patient consent. The same participating facility may provide its own records and request records from elsewhere. [National Resource Centre for EHR Standards ABDM FHIR guide](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/), [NHA ABDM wrapper](https://github.com/NHA-ABDM/ABDM-wrapper) |

Kerala's ABDM dashboard reports **ABHA–UHID linkings**, confirming that the identifiers can be linked in that implementation. This does not make the two numbers interchangeable or prove that a private clinic can retrieve eHealth Kerala data. The dashboard's figures also do not reveal whether the proposed clinic or any specific prior-record source is connected. [Kerala ABDM dashboard](https://dashboard.ehealth.kerala.gov.in/index.php/Init/ABDMdash), [eHealth Kerala portal](https://ehealth.kerala.gov.in/)

## Integration routes for this product

The following are **proposed architecture choices**, not existing capabilities of the potential clinic.

1. **Pilot: work alongside the clinic's current record system.** Capture the patient's paper documents at check-in, retain page-level provenance, build the doctor-facing brief, and let the doctor approve selected facts for today's note. Determine the clinic's actual EHR/HMIS vendor, if any, before building a connector. If the system supports an approved API or export/import interface, use that for patient/visit matching and clinician-approved note transfer. If it does not, test a doctor-approved PDF or structured export workflow. These interfaces and write permissions must be verified with the clinic and vendor; Kerala eHealth and ABHA identifiers alone do not provide them. The paper-first pilot follows the agreed [walk-in brief](pre-visit-record-brief.md).
2. **Later: receive ABDM records as an HIU.** A clinic-supported integration could verify/link the patient's ABHA identity, request the required consent through ABDM, and retrieve *available* records from participating HIPs. The imported record should retain its holder, document date, type, consent scope, and source document so the doctor can inspect it. NHA's integration guide describes milestone 1 as ABHA creation/verification, milestone 2 as linking and exporting records, and milestone 3 as consent requests and importing records. This route requires ABDM integration and testing; it is not a simple read-by-ABHA API. [NHA ABDM wrapper](https://github.com/NHA-ABDM/ABDM-wrapper), [National Resource Centre for EHR Standards ABDM FHIR guide](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/)
3. **Later: publish clinic records as an HIP.** If the clinic wants its completed consultations or captured historical documents available through ABDM, the responsible software must link shareable care contexts and serve compliant records after appropriate consent. The ABDM FHIR R4 guide defines profiles for outpatient consultations, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, discharge summaries, and unstructured historical health documents. A generated AI brief should remain distinguishable from original documents and a doctor-authored consultation record. [NHA ABDM wrapper](https://github.com/NHA-ABDM/ABDM-wrapper), [ABDM FHIR implementation guide](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/)

The National Health Authority publishes an ABDM wrapper with sample HIP and HIU applications, consent and health-information exchange modules, and sandbox callback examples. Its code is a technical starting point, not proof that this product or clinic is certified or live. The current endpoint, onboarding, security-audit, and production-access requirements should be checked in the [official ABDM sandbox](https://sandbox.abdm.gov.in/) when implementation begins; sandbox access was not available for direct inspection during this research. [NHA ABDM wrapper](https://github.com/NHA-ABDM/ABDM-wrapper)

The [ABDM FHIR R4 implementation guide](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/) is the standards reference for exchanged clinical artifacts. Its `HealthDocumentRecord` profile covers unstructured historical documents uploaded by patients; `OPConsultRecord`, `PrescriptionRecord`, and `DiagnosticReportRecord` cover different authored records. Map this product's internal clinical-note JSON to the relevant FHIR profile only after deciding which *approved* record the clinic is exporting. Do not assume the existing JSON schemas already conform to ABDM FHIR. [ABDM FHIR implementation guide](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/)

## What not to assume

- An ABHA number does **not** imply that all paper records have been digitized, linked, or made available by HIPs. This follows from ABDM's federated, consent-based exchange design and the separate patient-upload workflow described in its FHIR guide. [NHA privacy and security explanation](https://abdm.gov.in/static/media/Session%206%20-%20Digital%20health%20-%20Privacy%20%26%20Security.083a5b3e73ba533e321b.pdf), [ABDM FHIR implementation guide](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/)
- eHealth Kerala's public-facing services are described for enabled **government hospitals**. A direct eHealth Kerala API for an independent private clinic, or access to a patient's Kerala UHID record by such a clinic, is **unverified**. Seek written technical and data-access guidance from the [State Digital Health Mission contact listed by eHealth Kerala](https://ehealth.kerala.gov.in/) before designing that connection. [eHealth Kerala portal](https://ehealth.kerala.gov.in/)
- ABDM's Health Facility Registry includes private clinics, but registry inclusion is not evidence that a particular clinic has registered, connected software, published records, or obtained authorization to request data. [eHealth Kerala ABDM page](https://www.ehealth.kerala.gov.in/abdm), [NHA ABDM wrapper](https://github.com/NHA-ABDM/ABDM-wrapper)
- The user's impression that most clinics are unaware of ABDM is a **hypothesis**, not a measured adoption rate. Ask the pilot doctor and clinic administrator what software they use and whether they currently create ABHA-linked records; do not infer local adoption from a state or national count. [eHealth Kerala ABDM dashboard](https://dashboard.ehealth.kerala.gov.in/index.php/Init/ABDMdash)

## Questions to resolve with the clinic and vendors

1. Does the clinic use paper charts, an EHR/HMIS, or both? What product and version, and who controls its data and API access?
2. Is the clinic registered in HFR and does it already create or link ABHA records? Is its software ABDM-integrated as a HIP, HIU, or neither?
3. Can the existing system receive clinician-approved historical facts or consultation notes through a supported interface? Can it export visit/patient identifiers and authored notes for reconciliation?
4. During a walk-in visit, can a patient complete ABDM consent in time, and do their useful prior records appear from connected HIPs? Test with permission; the answer determines whether ABDM retrieval adds value to this paper-first pilot.
5. Who is authorized to contact Kerala eHealth about private-clinic access, and what formal interface and data-sharing terms, if any, does it offer?

**Decision for the pilot:** keep EHR and ABDM integration as designed extension points, but judge the first version on paper-record capture, source-linked clinical review, and measured doctor-time savings. Add each external connector only after the clinic's actual system, permissions, and available records are confirmed.
